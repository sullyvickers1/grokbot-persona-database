# Code of Conduct

Be specific. Be civil. Review the work.

## Expected

- Critique prompts, schema, and examples — not people.
- Assume good faith until the work shows otherwise.
- No harassment, slurs, or targeted abuse.
- No dumping generated slop and demanding a merge.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close PRs, lock threads, or block accounts that ignore this. Report abuse to the repository owner.
